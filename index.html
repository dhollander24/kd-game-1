    How to play
    • Goal: knock the other castle (the keep) from 10 health to 0.
    • 1. Spell a word from your 7 letter blocks. Its points become energy. Words of 5 or more letters get +3. Can't find one? Swap your blocks, but you earn nothing that turn.
    • Long words: 6 letters earns a free swordsman. 7 letters earns a free swordsman and TNT. Free items keep until you use them.
    • Special words earn a free item: SWORD, BLADE, KNIGHT (swordsman); BOW, ARROW, SHOOT (archer); WALL, STONE, ROCK (wall); BOOM, BOMB, FIRE (TNT). LOVE, HEART, KISS and HUG heal your castle by 2. There are a few more to find.
    • 2. Spend energy: wall (3) on your own land, swordsman (6) or archer (7) beside your castle, or TNT (6). Up to 10 unused energy carries over.
    • 3. Move and attack. Tap a troop: green squares are moves, red are targets. It can move and then attack once, but attacking ends its turn. New troops wait a turn.
    • Swordsman: 4 health, hits for 2 on the next square, moves 3. Archer: 2 health, hits for 1 up to 3 squares away, moves 1. Archers shoot over walls.
    • TNT lands within 3 squares of your castle or 2 of your troops. It does 3 damage there and 1 to each square beside it, including your own things.
    • Diamonds in the middle column pay +2 energy at the start of your turn for each of your troops standing on them.
    • Going second? You start with 4 energy to make up for it.
    • Unknown words go to the other player to allow or reject.
